### Why do you want to study your chosen major specifically at Georgia Tech?

My interest in computer science began in high school and I have since been nurturing it at the University of Minnesota for the past year and half. Through diverse coursework, research, and application of my CS skills in designing programs to improve my school community, I have become especially interested in the fields of natural language processing (NLP), AI and machine learning.  At my current institution, I have exhausted all available courses in my interest areas, including graduate-level classes. As such, I am seeking greater opportunities at Georgia Tech to further my knowledge in my academic passions. 

Discovering my passion for Natural Language Processing (NLP) was a serendipitous moment that transformed my academic pursuits. While developing an AI chatbot for the Chinese Student Community, I encountered unexpected challenges that piqued my curiosity about the complexities of language models. For example, sometimes my chatbot was unable to respond to certain questions that I had provided accurate answers to in the database, or it would only respond to questions with concise answers but unable to expand the answers in detail. I hope that by utilizing the recent advances in NLP, I can build AI assistants that can help people with various challenges and difficulties in their lives. As such, I seek opportunities that would deepen my understanding and expertise in NLP.

The wide array of research initiatives and applications at ML@GT Labs resonates deeply with my goal to refine the interaction between humans and machines to a level of seamless communication. Being part of such a dynamic research community is precisely the platform I seek to elevate my academic and research pursuits. Joining GIT's Computer Science program represents more than an academic endeavor; it symbolizes my commitment to contributing to a future where technology enhances human communication in profound and meaningful ways.

Since a young age, I’ve been inspired by my family's legacy of resilience and pursuit of excellence. Despite battling ADHD, I came up with various creative learning methods to extend my focus and maximize my intake of new information. With determination, I quickly excelled in all my classes, eventually propelling me to join honors classes reserved for the top 10% in academics. I look forward to further my pursuit of excellence at Georgia Tech.
