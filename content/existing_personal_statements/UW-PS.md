### Personal Statement
My interest in computer science began in high school and I have since been nurturing it at the University of Minnesota for the past year and half. Through diverse coursework, research, and application of my CS skills in designing programs to improve my school community, I have become especially interested in the fields of natural language processing (NLP) and AI.  At my current institution, I have completed available courses in my interest areas, including graduate-level classes. As such, I am seeking greater opportunities at a new institution to further my knowledge in my academic passions. 

Recently I developed an AI chatbot to help international students navigate the challenges of their new learning environment. This endeavor not only honed my technical skills but also sparked a vision to create a personalized AI assistant that can unlock individual motivation and potential, underscoring my passion for making technology accessible and meaningful to everyone. I hope that by utilizing the recent advances in NLP, I can build AI assistants that can help people with various challenges and difficulties in their lives.

UW's robust offerings in NLP, particularly through courses like CSEP 447 and 517 and the opportunity to engage with cutting-edge research at H2Lab and the Allen Institute for AI, align perfectly with my academic and career goals. These resources, coupled with UW's collaboration with industry giants like Microsoft and Google, present an unparalleled opportunity for me to delve deeper into AI research and contribute to the advancement of generative AI models. 

I have completed all prerequisite courses needed for UW to major in computer science.  As a matter of fact, I have exhausted the advanced AI, machine learning, and NLP offerings at my current institution, setting a strong foundation for my studies at UW. Should the highly competitive nature of the CS program at UW necessitate a second-choice major, I am equally passionate about pursuing data science, aiming to further my exploration in NLP within this field.

A pivotal chapter in my educational journey has been navigating the complexities of ADHD, a condition that presented unique challenges but also opportunities for growth and innovation. My struggle with ADHD was not just about managing attention spans but also about redefining my approach to learning and problem-solving. This was most evident during my preparation for the SAT, a period that tested my limits and ultimately showcased my resilience.

The SAT, with its lengthy passages and multiple-choice questions, posed a significant challenge. My ADHD made it difficult to retain information long enough to connect questions with their respective answers. After reading a question, I would forget the options; after reading the options, I'd lose track of the question or passage. This cycle of forgetfulness made traditional test-taking strategies ineffective for me.

Undeterred, I dedicated myself to finding a solution. Recognizing that conventional methods were not suited to my learning style, I embarked on a rigorous two-month journey, experimenting with various unconventional strategies. I transformed my study routine into an intensive exploration of what worked best for my unique needs. This included studying all day and adapting to sleeping only four hours a night, a testament to my commitment to overcoming this hurdle.

One of the most effective strategies I developed was performing SAT questions while standing and shouting the answers out loud. This kinetic and auditory engagement helped me anchor my thoughts and maintain focus on the task at hand. Recognizing the impracticality of these methods in a test environment, I adapted them to fit the SAT's constraints. During the exam, I visualized myself standing and moving my lips as if shouting the answers. This imaginative approach allowed me to simulate the conditions under which I had practiced, facilitating a better recall and concentration.

This period of my life was not just about preparing for an exam; it was a profound journey of self-discovery and adaptation. By confronting the challenges posed by ADHD head-on, I developed personalized strategies that leveraged my strengths and mitigated my weaknesses. These experiences have imbued me with a deep appreciation for the diverse ways in which people learn and the importance of tailoring educational approaches to individual needs.

I believe that my unique background and aspirations can enrich the diverse and inclusive community at the University of Washington. Coming from a family that embodies resilience and ambition and having navigated the challenges of ADHD and educational systems across different cultures, I bring a perspective that values determination, innovation, and the power of education to transform lives. It is my deepest desire to use my computer science knowledge to advance our world through providing greater access and opportunities to those with diverse challenges and inequalities. I truly see no better place than the University of Washington to nourish my education and career development.
