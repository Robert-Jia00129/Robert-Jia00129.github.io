Explain your interest in the major you selected. Describe how you have recently developed this interest, inside or outside of the classroom, and how this major relates to your professional goals. 


I have always been interested in helping people, particularly through understanding their needs and challenges, and then finding ways to motivate them to achieve their goals.  At the same time, I’m also interested in working with emerging technology to help many people more efficiently, as technology can quickly record and categorize information, then generate solutions based on programed data. I am drawn to the precision, structure, and logic of computer science and the innovative solutions it can bring to our lives. I think a much greater social impact could be achieved with today’s advancements in AI.  As such, I want to design AI products such as AI assistants to motivate people and help them tap into their potential. 

I have been nurturing my passion in CS at the University of Minnesota for the past year and half. Through diverse coursework, research, and application of my CS skills in designing programs to improve my school community, I have become especially interested in the fields of natural language processing (NLP) and AI.  At my current institution, I have exhausted available courses in my interest areas, including graduate-level classes. As such, I am seeking greater opportunities at a new institution to further my knowledge in my academic passions. 

Discovering my passion for Natural Language Processing (NLP) was a serendipitous moment that transformed my academic pursuits. While developing an AI chatbot for the Chinese Student Community, I encountered unexpected challenges that piqued my curiosity about the complexities of language models. For example, sometimes my chatbot was unable to respond to certain questions that I had provided accurate answers to in the database, or it would only respond to questions with concise answers but unable to expand the answers in detail. I hope that by utilizing recent advances in NLP, I can build AI assistants that can help people with various challenges in their lives. Furthermore, I want to create dynamic learning systems that adapt to each individual in real time learner's style, pace, and emotional state, making education more effective and engaging, not only in an academic setting, but also in personal life. 

My curiosity in AI has inspired me to conduct research in this field. Currently, I’m researching on using LLMs in information retrieval and techniques to help build an AI assistant that is more human like and can make people feel that they are really having a conversation with someone.
