### Common Application Essay 

My story is not just mine alone, but rather inspired by several members of my family. I feel proud to carry the torch of a family legacy defined by resilience, ambition, and the relentless pursuit of excellence. My grandfather, born from humble origins, became the first person in his town to make 10,000 RMB through repeated efforts of trying his business ideas. He had a fearless spirit to attempt the unknown and learn through doing. Following his footsteps, my mother shattered societal and educational barriers to become a trailblazer in her own right. She was the first student to ever graduate from her university and then went on to become the first person in her hometown to earn a PhD. She later worked as a chemist for Fortune 500 companies like P&G and Unilever. However, her ambitions to start her own worldwide company led her to create a unique line of natural beauty products. Despite being a scientist trying to run a business, my mom immersed herself in self-learning and tapped into her network for business advice.  She always found a way to turn failures into success and make the impossible possible. 

My grandfather and mother have been inspirational throughout my life. From them, I learned that I can always make the best out of my situation, work hard, and rise above it. I learned to not just dream but dream big. Then follow through with persistent effort.

My education journey began at an under-resourced primary school. Consequentially, many of my peers sought enrichment classes outside of school to get ahead in Math, Science, and English. However, I didn’t want my parents to spend the extra money, so I chose to teach myself using online resources. With determination to improve, I quickly excelled in all my classes, eventually propelling me to join honors classes which were reserved for the top 10% in academics. My ambition didn’t just stop there as I wanted to use my merits to get into a top high school in Shanghai. When that day came, I was delighted. However, my excitement didn’t last for long when I found myself unprepared for international school education which required much more critical thinking, presentations, and essay writing. So again, I began to play catch up. Meeting with my teachers after class to discuss new learning strategies and coming with improvement plans on my own. I worked tirelessly to improve my grades, resulting in one semester better than the one before. 

University life marked the beginning of a new chapter in my journey, one where the pursuit of knowledge became an unquenchable thirst. I’m constantly exploring my resources and learning opportunities like a goldmine. My passion for learning transcends the confines of a traditional academic calendar. Vacations are opportunities for intensive research, as I immerse myself in the depths of academia, often being the lone student on campus during breaks. However, when I am studying what I’m passionate about, my books become my companion, satisfying my ferocious appetite for more knowledge, and understanding how to use it to better serve individuals and communities. At same time, I aim to be like my grandfather and mother, continuously striving to push boundaries and reach new heights of excellence.
