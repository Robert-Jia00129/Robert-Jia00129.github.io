Working with Professor Joosten to conduct research on smt solvers. We first found that different approach in constraint building of the same problem leads to significantly different results. This motivates us to produce a platform that benchmarks different encoding techniques across different solvers and visualize their results. Submitted paper “JZ3: A Python Package for SMT Benchmarking” to SMT workshop on May 12, 2024.
