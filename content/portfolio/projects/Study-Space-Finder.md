**Project Overview:**
I led the development of the Study Space Finder, a mobile application designed to address the need for an updated and user-friendly tool to help students locate study spaces across campus. The existing website was outdated, particularly in terms of poor mobile compatibility, necessitating a much-needed overhaul.

**Timeline:**
The project commenced in September 2023 and is currently ongoing. Initially, we attempted to develop the app using Swift, but within a month, we realized its limitations. Swift’s restrictive paradigms and its susceptibility to complete failure from a single error made it a less viable option. Consequently, we switched to using React Native for better cross-platform compatibility and ease of development.

**Team Composition and Roles:**
The project initially involved nearly 10 team members, including:
- **Developers:** Worked on both frontend and backend development.
- **Database Administrators (DBAs):** Managed the complex access negotiations and handling of university data.
- **User Experience (UX) Designers:** Focused on creating an intuitive and user-friendly interface for the app.
- **Project Lead (Myself):** Initially struggled with leadership but later grew to effectively manage and guide the project. Responsibilities included coordinating team efforts, managing database access permissions, and overseeing development and design implementations.

**Challenges and Solutions:**
- **Database Access:** One of the most significant challenges was obtaining access to the university’s sensitive database, which required numerous applications and agreements to ensure compliance with university policies and data usage constraints.
- **Technology Switch:** The decision to switch from Swift to React Native was driven by Swift's complexity and high error susceptibility. React Native, being a more interactive and developer-friendly framework, facilitated smoother development and better mobile compatibility for both iOS and Android platforms.
- **Leadership Struggles:** Initially, my lack of leadership skills led to disorganization and inefficiency in managing team tasks, including database access, app design, and code implementation. However, this experience became a valuable lesson in leadership, teaching me to guide the team more effectively moving forward.

**User Feedback:**
As the app is still in the prototype stage, we have not yet collected user feedback. The focus has been on ensuring the functional aspects and interface are robust before reaching out for user testing.

**Outcomes and Impact:**
- **Ongoing Development:** The app is still under development, with significant progress made in building a more user-friendly and technologically robust tool compared to the outdated website.
- **Personal Growth:** The initial failure to lead the team effectively was a challenging experience but taught me critical lessons in leadership, project management, and resilience. This growth has been instrumental in restarting the project with stronger leadership and clearer direction.

### Key Takeaways and Personal Reflection:
- **Leadership Skills:** Initially struggling with leadership highlighted my need to develop better management and team coordination skills. This resulted in significant personal growth and a more effective approach in the latter stages of the project.
- **Technical Adaptability:** The ability to pivot from Swift to React Native demonstrates technical adaptability and a commitment to choosing the best tools for the project’s success.
- **Resilience and Learning:** Overcoming initial failures instilled a valuable lesson in resilience and the importance of learning from setbacks to drive future successes.

By focusing on these aspects and incorporating ongoing feedback and testing phases, the Study Space Finder app is poised to become an integral tool for students, enhancing their study experience with an efficient and user-friendly solution.
