### Detailed Project Description: Creating a Centralized Information Website for New Students

**Project Overview:**
Recognizing the difficulties students face when they first arrive at the university, particularly in navigating scattered resources, I spearheaded a project to create a centralized information website, https://www.mingcns.org/. This site consolidates all necessary information into one accessible place, significantly easing the transition process for newcomers.

**Timeline:**
The project commenced upon identifying the need for a centralized hub of information, and the initial setup was relatively quick as it involved developing a static website. The main effort was focused on gathering and documenting exhaustive and relevant information.

**Roles and Contributions:**
My role was multifaceted:
1. **Content Gathering and Documentation:** Utilizing my own experiences and systematically collecting information from various resources.
2. **Website Development:** Although the website was static, it required meticulous attention to detail to ensure information was well-organized and easily navigable.
3. **Community Engagement:** We set up a Google form on the website to continuously gather insights and experiences from users, ensuring the content remains current and comprehensive.

I collaborated with experienced seniors in web development for site architecture and design elements.

**Technologies and Tools:**
- **Web Development:** The website was built using basic web technologies (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript).
- **Data Collection:** Google Forms was employed to gather ongoing user contributions and feedback.
- **Social Media Expansion:** To broaden our reach, we utilized "RED," a popular Chinese social media platform known for information sharing.

**Survey Methodology:**
To supplement publicly available information, we conducted surveys via Google Forms to collect personal experiences and opinions on topics such as wireless companies, apartments, and canteens. This mixed-method approach ensured comprehensive coverage of student needs.

**Challenges and Solutions:**
- **Challenge:** Ensuring accuracy and relevance of the information.
  **Solution:** Regular updates and user contributions via Google Forms help maintain data integrity. 

- **Challenge:** Reaching a broader audience.
  **Solution:** Expanding onto social media platforms like RED amplified our reach and engagement.

**Personal Reflection:**
Throughout this project, I developed several key skills:
- **Problem-Solving and Initiative:** Identifying a significant gap in information accessibility and creating a solution.
- **Collaboration:** Working effectively with peers in web development and engaging the wider student community.
- **Technical Skills:** Enhancing my web development and data organization capabilities.
- **Community Engagement:** Learning to gather, analyze, and incorporate user feedback continually.

**Outcomes and Impact:**
The website has become an invaluable resource for new students, reducing anxiety by providing a reliable starting point for finding information. It also serves as a comprehensive guide for settled students seeking more detailed insights without extensive searching.

**User Feedback:**
Feedback has been overwhelmingly positive, with users appreciating the ease of access to consolidated information and the reliability of the resources provided. Specific comments highlighted the stress reduction and time-saving benefits of having a one-stop information hub.

**Significant Achievement:**
The most notable achievement of this project is the positive impact on the student community, exemplified by the continuous use and contribution to the website. It has not only facilitated smoother transitions for new students but also fostered a sense of community involvement and support.
