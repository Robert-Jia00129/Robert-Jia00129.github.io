**Objective:**
The project aimed to create a WeChat mini-program to facilitate a more efficient and structured way for students to post and find information related to sub-leasing, finding activity partners, selling second-hand items, and organizing events. The goal was to address and resolve the common issues of information overload, poor filtering, and management challenges commonly experienced in traditional group chats.

**Key Events:**

1. **Identifying the Problem:**
   - Students experienced difficulties in group chats due to information overload, lack of filtering options, and the challenge of managing multiple chats.
   - Recognizing the need for a more effective medium for sharing specific types of information.

2. **Designing the Solution:**
   - Conceptualized a WeChat mini-program to address these challenges.
   - Outlined key functionalities, including categories for sub-leasing, activity partners, second-hand sales, and event organization.

3. **Development Phase:**
   - Utilized a general framework for the basic functionalities and customized it to meet specific needs of the school's student community.
   - Developed the initial version of the mini-program with another computer science senior focusing on technological aspects.
   - Tools and technologies: General frameworks for WeChat mini-programs, custom coding, and adaptation for the unique needs of the user base.

4. **Initial User Feedback and Iteration:**
   - Released the initial version to the student community.
   - Faced considerable critical feedback primarily on various bugs in the program.
   - Addressed and fixed the bugs, then relaunched the program successfully.
   - Post-feedback iterations focused on usability improvements and bug fixes, leading to a highly convenient and efficient platform.

5. **Manual Moderation and Content Management:**
   - Implemented manual moderation to manage content. Inaccurate or inappropriate content was deleted manually through a central management account.
   - Explored automated content moderation but faced integration challenges due to WeChat's platform restrictions.

6. **Promotion Strategy:**
   - Minimal advertising was needed beyond initial text promotions highlighting the mini-program's advantages over group chats.
   - Relied on the inherent benefits and word-of-mouth to drive user adoption.

**Challenges and Overcoming Them:**
   - **User Interface Design:** Ensuring the interface was user-friendly and intuitive.
   - **Data Handling:** Making the platform handle large amounts of information efficiently.
   - **Adoption Encouragement:** Convincing students to transition from traditional group chats.
   - **Content Moderation:** Managing content manually to ensure quality and appropriateness.
   - **Feedback Management:** Handling critical initial feedback constructively and utilizing it to improve the product.

**Roles and Contributions:**
   - Initially, two team members and I focused on the technological build.
   - After their graduation, I took over full responsibility, including technology maintenance, content management, account management (renewing app certificates), and promoting the platform.

**Personal Insights and Skills Developed:**
   - Gained a deeper understanding of app development, including workflow, major components, and UI design.
   - Developed management skills, including content moderation and app certificate renewal.
   - Honed promotional skills to effectively communicate the app's benefits to potential users.
   - Overcame a significant challenge in relaunching the app after initial failure, showcasing resilience and the ability to handle criticism constructively.

**Outcomes:**
   - Drastically improved the organization and accessibility of information for students.
   - Reduced clutter in group chats, enabling students to quickly find relevant information.
   - Provided a sustainable and effective platform for various student needs, from sub-leasing to finding event partners.

### Suggested Next Steps for Continuous Improvement (if relevant):
   - Exploring further AI and automated moderation tools to integrate within WeChat mini-program constraints.
   - Seeking continuous user feedback to make iterative improvements.
   - Expanding promotion strategies to ensure new students are aware of and using the mini-program.
   - Exploring additional features that could benefit the student community.
