### Comprehensive Project Description: Gopher Notes

**Project Overview:**
Gopher Notes was founded with the mission of enhancing academic collaboration among students at the University of Minnesota. We developed a web platform designed to serve as a repository where students can share and access course materials, including notes, syllabi, and slides, thereby assisting students in preparing for their courses and enabling those currently enrolled to catch up more efficiently. A unique feature of Gopher Notes is its points-based leaderboard system, which motivates and rewards students for their contributions. The platform is currently in its prototype stage and is showing promising potential for widespread use among the student body.

**Motivations for Creating the Website:**
The inspiration for Gopher Notes stemmed from my personal experience as an avid note-taker and informal tutor for my classmates. Throughout my academic journey, I observed several recurring issues:
- **Accessibility:** Many students, including those with special circumstances or disabilities, faced significant challenges in attending in-person classes and subsequently needed reliable notes to catch up. However, it was not feasible for me to take notes for every class at the university.
- **Course Selection:** Students frequently struggled to find comprehensive course materials before selecting classes. There was a desire to review course content to make informed decisions.
- **Sharing Medium:** Although there were students who took detailed notes and were willing to share them, there lacked a centralized platform to facilitate this exchange. 

**Timeline and Development:**
The development of Gopher Notes commenced in September 2023. After nearly a year of careful planning, coding, and testing, a prototype was successfully developed. We aim to move beyond the prototype stage and launch the full platform around September 2024.

**Team and Roles:**
The core development team consists of two members: myself and another experienced computer science major student. Initially, the project started with a larger team of over 15 people, but due to inefficiencies and stagnation, the group was streamlined down to just the two of us.

**Technical Tools and Technologies:**
For the development of Gopher Notes, we utilized the following technologies:
- **Front End:** TypeScript and React
- **Back End:** Rust (using the maximum crate)
- **Database Management:** MySQL

**Key Decisions and Motivations:**
We incorporated a points-based leaderboard system after extensive research on motivation systems. Our main goal was to achieve sustainable engagement from users, bypassing the challenges of early-stage adoption with minimal content. We concluded that leveraging human feelings of recognition and achievement would provide a more sustainable model than physical rewards, which are difficult to maintain and can detract from intrinsic motivation. Our research was reinforced by the success of platforms like Wikipedia and StackOverflow, which rely on similar psychological mechanisms.

**Skills and Learning Experiences:**
Throughout the project, several skills were honed and developed:
- **Technical Skills:** Advanced front-end and back-end development, database management, and deployment of web applications.
- **Personal Skills:** Improved project management, leadership, effective collaboration in small teams, and problem-solving during periods of stagnation.
- **Insights Gained:** A significant realization was that a smaller, more dedicated team could operate more efficiently than a larger, less coordinated group. This insight came after the initial larger team proved ineffective, leading us to continue with just two members, resulting in rapid progress.

**Tangible Outcomes and Beta Testing:**
Currently, the platform requires a substantial amount of materials to be fully functional, which has limited tangible outcomes thus far. However, we have released the site to a small group of beta users and are actively collecting their feedback. The initial response has been optimistic, providing useful insights for further improvements.

**Next Steps and Future Launch:**
Looking forward, we expect a full launch of Gopher Notes by September 2024. The next steps involve incorporating beta user feedback, populating the platform with more course materials, and refining the user experience.
