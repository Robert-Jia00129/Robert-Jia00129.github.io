ACSL (American Computer Science League): Team Leader & Team Silver

ACSL Individual:  All-Stars	

I was the leader of a team of three for this contest. None of us had any prior programming experience. So I taught myself all the competition materials, and organized weekly sessions to teach my members as well. I enjoyed the sense of achievement when resolving a hard question.

This is my first experience with programming. I really enjoyed the process while preparing for this competition together with my peers. One of the greatest outcomes of this competition is the ability to effectively acquire information online and selt-teaching skills.
