IGEM(International Genetically Engineered Machine Competition)  
Undergraduate Division: Team Silver


IGEM is a competition where students utilize their expert knowledge of biology to tackle real-world problems. We assessed the potential risk of lateral transfer of E. coli plasmids by T4 bacteriophages under different conditions. Hoping to evaluate and quantify the risks that lateral gene transfer poses to the environment. We also devised adaptive lab equipment for those with low-vision, no vision, or limited motor control. I am also responsible for programming our final webpage for the competition.
