This research project was inspired by professor Pavlos Protopapas's teaching. I was the leader of a team of four. Despite being the only high school student and knowing very little about artificial intelligence, I did not fear leading the team. I intended to establish an engaging and constructive learning environment and deemed this crucial for a long-term project like this. I devoted all of my time to this program. Eventually, becoming one of the two main coding members and the main paper-writing member.
