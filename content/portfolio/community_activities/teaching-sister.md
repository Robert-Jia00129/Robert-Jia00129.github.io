# Helping My Sister Excel During the COVID-19 Pandemic: A Journey of Growth and Determination

## Background
During the challenging times of the COVID-19 pandemic, my sister faced significant obstacles in her academic journey. As she prepared for the primary school entrance exams, she struggled with language, art, logical reasoning, and math. These subjects became formidable barriers, and with our mother deeply absorbed in her business, which had been severely impacted by the pandemic, the responsibility of guiding my sister fell squarely on my shoulders.

## Creating a Structured Approach
Recognizing the importance of instilling strong study habits, I decided to take a structured approach. I created a goal-oriented timeline, with the ultimate aim of fostering lifelong learning habits in my sister. While gaining admission to top schools was a goal, I believed that this achievement should merely be a byproduct of the solid foundation we were building together.

## Innovative Strategies and Overcoming Challenges
One of the most effective strategies I employed was to invite my sister to study alongside me. This approach not only cultivated a focused and positive learning environment but also demonstrated to her that studying was not a solitary endeavor. By sharing this experience, she could see that I, too, was committed to learning and not just imposing it on her. Over time, this mutual dedication transformed studying from a chore into a shared journey.


At the start, we faced considerable challenges. My sister was reluctant to engage in studying, and I struggled to find the right resources and activities to capture her interest. Moreover, balancing this responsibility with my own online classes was demanding. The initial phase required intense effort as I had to teach her the very basics, but as she began to find joy in learning, our study sessions became more interactive and less burdensome. The turning point came when she developed self-learning skills, enabling us to work independently yet together.

## Managing Stress and Encouraging Growth
To help her manage stress, I incorporated physical activities, created more engaging study sessions, and provided constant positive feedback. Listening to her feelings and desires was crucial in maintaining her motivation. Although she was too young to fully grasp the long-term implications of the pandemic and her education, I explained how our daily efforts were improving her temperament and overall well-being, especially in comparison to the passive experience of spending hours on an iPad.

## Achieving Success and Reflecting on the Journey
The year of consistent effort paid off. My sister not only passed her exams but excelled, demonstrating the strength of her character and the effectiveness of our approach. While I provided the structure and guidance, it was her hard work and perseverance that led to her success. I felt immense pride, not just in her accomplishments, but in the remarkable transformation I witnessed—seeing her evolve from needing constant attention to becoming a confident and independent learner was truly rewarding.
