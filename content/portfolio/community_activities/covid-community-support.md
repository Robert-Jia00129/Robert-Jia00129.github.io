During Shanghai's three-month COVID- 19 lockdown, our neighborhood grappled with severe food and supply shortages. To assist, me, along with a few neighbors, hope that we could provide support to our community. We distributed essential supplies and packed bread and egg. Additionally, we donated toiletries to families, utilizing samples from my mother's company.
