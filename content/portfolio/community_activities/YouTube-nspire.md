In an effort to empower students in their AP Calculus journey, I took the initiative to create and develop a comprehensive series of tutorial videos on YouTube. This project was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between complex calculus concepts and the practical application of technology in problem-solving. The tutorials are meticulously designed to teach students how to effectively use the Ti-nspire graphing calculator, a powerful tool often underutilized in exam settings.

My approach was both methodical and student-centered, ensuring that each video not only covered all the essential topics tested in Calculus exams where a graphing calculator could be advantageous but also provided step-by-step instructions. Through these tutorials, I aimed to enhance students' problem-solving skills by offering practical tips and strategies, making complex problems more approachable and less intimidating.

This project showcases my ability to identify educational needs and create resources that are both accessible and impactful. My commitment to helping others succeed is evident in the careful planning and execution of this series. The attention to detail in each tutorial reflects my dedication to clarity and precision, ensuring that students can follow along easily and apply what they learn effectively.

Furthermore, this initiative underscores my ability to leverage technology for educational purposes, demonstrating both technical proficiency with the Ti-nspire graphing calculator and the capability to translate complex concepts into digestible content. Through this project, I have not only shared valuable knowledge but also exhibited my passion for teaching, my commitment to academic excellence, and my drive to make a meaningful impact on students' learning experiences.
