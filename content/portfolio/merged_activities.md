# Helping My Sister Excel During the COVID-19 Pandemic: A Journey of Growth and Determination

## Background
During the challenging times of the COVID-19 pandemic, my sister faced significant obstacles in her academic journey. As she prepared for the primary school entrance exams, she struggled with language, art, logical reasoning, and math. These subjects became formidable barriers, and with our mother deeply absorbed in her business, which had been severely impacted by the pandemic, the responsibility of guiding my sister fell squarely on my shoulders.

## Creating a Structured Approach
Recognizing the importance of instilling strong study habits, I decided to take a structured approach. I created a goal-oriented timeline, with the ultimate aim of fostering lifelong learning habits in my sister. While gaining admission to top schools was a goal, I believed that this achievement should merely be a byproduct of the solid foundation we were building together.

## Innovative Strategies and Overcoming Challenges
One of the most effective strategies I employed was to invite my sister to study alongside me. This approach not only cultivated a focused and positive learning environment but also demonstrated to her that studying was not a solitary endeavor. By sharing this experience, she could see that I, too, was committed to learning and not just imposing it on her. Over time, this mutual dedication transformed studying from a chore into a shared journey.


At the start, we faced considerable challenges. My sister was reluctant to engage in studying, and I struggled to find the right resources and activities to capture her interest. Moreover, balancing this responsibility with my own online classes was demanding. The initial phase required intense effort as I had to teach her the very basics, but as she began to find joy in learning, our study sessions became more interactive and less burdensome. The turning point came when she developed self-learning skills, enabling us to work independently yet together.

## Managing Stress and Encouraging Growth
To help her manage stress, I incorporated physical activities, created more engaging study sessions, and provided constant positive feedback. Listening to her feelings and desires was crucial in maintaining her motivation. Although she was too young to fully grasp the long-term implications of the pandemic and her education, I explained how our daily efforts were improving her temperament and overall well-being, especially in comparison to the passive experience of spending hours on an iPad.

## Achieving Success and Reflecting on the Journey
The year of consistent effort paid off. My sister not only passed her exams but excelled, demonstrating the strength of her character and the effectiveness of our approach. While I provided the structure and guidance, it was her hard work and perseverance that led to her success. I felt immense pride, not just in her accomplishments, but in the remarkable transformation I witnessed—seeing her evolve from needing constant attention to becoming a confident and independent learner was truly rewarding.

# Robert's Journey in the UKO Club and the Creation of the Rust Subgroup

## UKO Club Overview

The University Kernel Object (UKO) Club is dedicated to exploring and enhancing the Linux operating system's efficiency and security, primarily through translating C code into Rust. During his first semester, Robert immersed himself in the club's activities, significantly deepening his technical acumen and understanding of operating system architecture and programming languages. His participation marked a critical phase in his educational journey, fostering a collaborative atmosphere with a shared goal of advancing system efficiency.

## Subgroup Creation

Robert identified several gaps in the UKO Club's pedagogical approach, particularly in teaching Rust to a diverse group of programmers. The existing methodology, which relied heavily on self-study from the Rust book followed by a Rust game project, was effective for seasoned programmers but overwhelming for beginners. Recognizing this disparity, Robert decided to create a Rust-focused subgroup within the UKO Club. His motivation was to bridge the gap between novices and advanced programmers by devising a more inclusive and effective learning strategy.

## Challenges and Solutions

### **Key Challenges:**

1. **Learning Curve of Rust:**
   - **Issue:** Rust's steep learning curve made it difficult for beginners to grasp fundamental concepts.
   - **Solution:** Robert structured the semester to focus entirely on Rust education, making it accessible irrespective of prior experience. He created a balanced curriculum that demystified Rust for newcomers while providing depth for experienced members.

2. **Inaccessible Lectures:**
   - **Issue:** The lectures, though passionate and expert-driven, were too domain-specific, alienating beginners.
   - **Solution:** Robert proposed a bifurcated lecture system, splitting content into sections for both novices and advanced learners. This approach catered to the varied expertise levels within the group.

3. **Mismatch Between Objectives and Implementation:**
   - **Issue:** The club's advanced curriculum did not align well with the final project of creating an educational Rust game, resulting in a lack of engagement.
   - **Solution:** Robert introduced a meticulously planned weekly lecture schedule, offering flexible attendance options. This restructured approach ensured continuous progress while accommodating individual schedules.

### **Implementation Plan:**

- **Weekly Lecture Schedule:**
    ```
    Tuesday         Thursday
    Week1:                    L1
    Week2:      L1            L2
    Week3:      L2            L3
    ...
    ```
  - **Flexibility:** Students had the option to attend sessions on both Tuesday and Thursday for accelerated learning or to fit their personal routines.
  - **Buffering:** Attending both sessions provided a built-in buffer, allowing students to miss one session without falling behind.

- **Lecture Content and Delivery:**
  - **Segmentation:** Lectures consisted of two parts—basic concepts for beginners in the first 45 minutes and advanced topics for experienced participants in the remaining time.
  - **Interactive Methods:** Robert incorporated historical anecdotes and practical examples to make the material engaging and relatable.

## Skill Development

Throughout this process, Robert cultivated and demonstrated a variety of skills:

- **Leadership:** He took initiative by identifying issues and proposing comprehensive solutions, leading the subgroup with a clear vision.
- **Organizational Abilities:** Robert meticulously planned the lecture schedule and structured the curriculum to cater to different learning paces and levels of understanding.
- **Pedagogical Skills:** He developed and delivered accessible and engaging educational content, ensuring that it was understandable for all.

- **Technical Knowledge:** Robert not only reinforced his own understanding of Rust but also facilitated the learning of others through structured and interactive teaching methods.
- **Creativity:** By integrating historical context and practical examples into lectures, he made complex topics more accessible and interesting.

## Impact and Future Plans

Robert's contributions had a profound impact on the UKO Club, making Rust education more inclusive and effective. His efforts ensured a sustainable model for future learning sessions, balancing the needs of both novice and advanced programmers. Moving forward, Robert plans to further develop the subgroup by continuously refining the curriculum and introducing new, engaging projects. These plans align with the UKO Club's overarching mission to enhance system efficiency and security through collaborative learning and innovative programming approaches. 

His journey highlights the significant personal and professional growth he achieved, demonstrating the ability to identify challenges, propose innovative solutions, and lead effectively.

### Comprehensive Project Description: Gopher Notes

**Project Overview:**
Gopher Notes was founded with the mission of enhancing academic collaboration among students at the University of Minnesota. We developed a web platform designed to serve as a repository where students can share and access course materials, including notes, syllabi, and slides, thereby assisting students in preparing for their courses and enabling those currently enrolled to catch up more efficiently. A unique feature of Gopher Notes is its points-based leaderboard system, which motivates and rewards students for their contributions. The platform is currently in its prototype stage and is showing promising potential for widespread use among the student body.

**Motivations for Creating the Website:**
The inspiration for Gopher Notes stemmed from my personal experience as an avid note-taker and informal tutor for my classmates. Throughout my academic journey, I observed several recurring issues:
